HOW IT WORKS

Three steps to a book.

01

Pick a cover, drop in one photo

Weekly, travel, picture book, fashion, life, food — choose a cover from the newsstand and add one photo. Masthead, lines and materials are yours to change.

02

Pick more, get pages

Pick several photos: the first is the cover, the rest are laid out like a scrapbook. Print borders, stickers, handwriting, tape — as you like.

03

Maps, routes, export

Pins for restaurants, a hand-drawn route for trips. Export the finished book as Instagram-sized images or a PDF.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Do my photos go to a server?

No. There is no account and no server. Everything is made on your phone. The only network traffic fetches map imagery when you open a map and confirms purchases.

Does it make albums automatically?

No. Google Photos and Apple Photos already do that well. Gallory is where you make one book from photos you chose. We lay out a draft; the layout, words and materials are yours.

How far does free go?

A single cover is free all the way — make it, export it, share it. Pro is needed only to save and export a book with inside pages. Decorating is always free.

Which languages?

Korean, English, Japanese, Spanish and Portuguese, with Korean and Japanese serif masthead fonts included.
